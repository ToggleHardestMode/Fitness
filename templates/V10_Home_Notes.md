# V10 Home — Adaptive Recovery Upper/Lower (21 October 2026)

## Status and intended use

Provisional home dumbbell/adjustable-bench workout template, developed for a 90 kg adult seeking body recomposition and reporting persistent intermittent leg tingling/numbness after training.

**Medical limitation:** The [PT Review] Lower A and Lower B plans are **not medically cleared** by this app. Arrange examination of persistent radiating leg symptoms with a clinician/physiotherapist familiar with lumbar radicular pain before starting or progressing the lower-body plans. The app cannot assess safety. Stop provoking movements for sharp/radiating pain, new numbness or weakness; emergency symptoms include saddle numbness or new bladder/bowel problems.

## Equipment

Adjustable bench, home dumbbells, stable support, and floor. No gym machines, cables, resistance bands or pull-up bar assumed. If dumbbells are fixed 15–20 kg, **skip lateral raises/reverse flies/other exercises where load is too heavy** until suitably lighter resistance is available. Do not force unsuitable weights. If a bench/chair is too low for non-provocative sit-to-stand, do not force depth.

## Recovery, progression, symptom monitoring

- Upper 2–3 RIR. Lower 3–4 RIR **after clinician screening**, progressing one variable at a time.
- Warm up with comfortable light activity and easy specific ramp sets. Lower day bird-dogs only if tolerated and clinician agrees.
- Readiness 8–10 normal plan; 6–7 train conservatively; 4–5 reduce sets ~30%; 3 or below rest/review. Readiness **never overrides symptoms**.
- Log before, during, after, next morning, and up to 48 h if symptomatic: location of leg sensation (buttock/thigh/calf/toes), intensity, tingling/numbness, function and exercise exposures.
- If symptoms worsen or travel farther down the leg after training, **do not increase** the suspected exercise; reduce/omit it and get clinical advice.
- In comfortable exercises, build reps at a fixed load before increasing weights. For lower body, repeat stable doses and progress only after review.
- App workout imports do not automatically assign workouts, change previous logs, or enforce clinician/symptom rules. Lower A/B names carry [PT Review]; they must stay unassigned until assessed.
- The timed side plank uses the rep field as **seconds per side** because this version of FitnessPilot lacks a duration field.
- `source_note` is informational within the source CSV; the current workout parser does **not** retain it. Consult this document during training.

## Body recomposition

Starting points at 90 kg: protein ~160–180 g/day; target a small calorie deficit (~250–400 kcal/day below **observed** maintenance) and aim for sustainable, symptom-tolerated activity. Diet and weight targets must adjust to actual trends and recovery.

## Import procedure

1. Tools → Download full backup.
2. Plan → **Add V10 Home templates**, or **Download V10 CSV**, then import via CSV controls.
3. Select Upper A/Upper B on Monday/Thursday, leaving the lower templates unassigned pending clinical assessment.
4. Save schedule. Track neurological symptom response **outside the app**, because this build does not implement a dedicated symptom tracker.
5. If a template already exists, importing again skips duplicates.

These templates are a training plan, not clinical treatment advice.
