Backup / restore

Food Diary uses its own local preview key. Existing workout and manual calorie/protein data are untouched. The workout-app backup does not include this separate diary.

Restore is blocked when any diary entries already exist; it never silently replaces or merges records. Store the exported file somewhere safe.

Reference/estimated food nutrients are not guaranteed actual intake. Incomplete food logs are not proof of meeting energy targets. No automatic calorie adjustment, food uploading or exercise-calorie subtraction.
