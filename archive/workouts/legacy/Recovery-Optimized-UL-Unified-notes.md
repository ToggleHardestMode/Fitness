## Existing workouts

The old split workout templates are not rewritten or deleted. Any logs recorded against them remain intact and can still be viewed. The new Unified templates have distinct names and must be added explicitly in **Plan → Add Unified Upper/Lower plan**. They are not assigned to weekdays automatically. Assign them in Plan when ready, keeping your older templates for history.

Importing the same Unified plan again skips duplicates. The optional CSV column `set_targets` uses one `minimum-maximum` range per set, separated by `|`. Existing CSV files without the column remain supported.

Before changing plans, use Tools → Download full backup.
