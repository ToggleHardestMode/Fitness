# FitnessPilot V10.1 — Batch 03 Training Analytics

Branch: `feature/v101-training-analytics-20261021` (based on Batch 02, PR #7).

## Changed behavior
- Dashboard differentiates weekly **scheduled sets** from **recorded working sets**, and identifies saved sets recorded outside current weekday assignments.
- Reports per-category **direct sets only**. `Arms` remains a combined unspecified label, not automatically split into biceps/triceps or credited for compound movements.
- Displays timed side-plank observations in **seconds per side** and excludes them from kg × reps and heaviest-load leaderboard.
- Reports recovery observations without suggesting medical clearance or load increases, with missing next-day responses shown as unknown.
- Keeps local-only storage and existing JSON backups unchanged; this feature is read-only.

## Scope limits
- Planned sets reflect **current schedule assignments**, not the historical plan assigned on a past date. Changing the weekly schedule may change the interpretation of a past week. Historical assignment snapshots are a later data-model change.
- The existing logger does not distinguish warm-up sets. Explicit `isWarmup:true` or `kind:'warmup'` tags are ignored in these calculations, but untagged legacy sets are treated as working sets. This is a limitation, not proven classification.
- Logging all scheduled sets does not prove a completed workout session.
- The current `Arms` label is not enough to give truthful separate biceps and triceps direct-set totals.
- Symptom comparisons require the same workout, exercise scope and workout-date record. Incomplete or absent entries are not evidence of safety.
- Current dashboard reviews the latest relevant day in the displayed week, not a long-term symptom history. The full journal remains available on Train.
- No lower-body progression, diagnoses, medical clearance, or sound/theme changes.
