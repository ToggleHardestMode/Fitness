# Research synthesis: high-protein, low-calorie Singapore foods
**Nature:** synthesis of the conversation's November 2026 food-price, label and dietary-method investigations. Research was not independently rerun for this archival commit.

## Decisions and working conclusions
- Use **protein per 100 kcal**, **cost per 30g protein**, and **micronutrient diversity** as separate optimization axes. Do not invent an overall "best" score.
- Lean plain chicken breast is a strong baseline for concentrated economical protein; tuna in brine is high-density but frequency, mercury and sodium matter; eggs, tau kwa, legumes, sardines, calcium-fortified soy and dairy fill other roles.
- The cheapest ingredient by weight may not be cheapest per digestible protein or edible yield. Explicitly account for bones, shells, frozen-to-cooked yields, drained canned contents, delivery fees and full-pack purchases.
- Deficit-based weight loss does not require a special macronutrient dogma or fasting window. Adequate protein, vegetable/fruit diversity, fiber, some unsaturated fats, resistance training and adherence remain central.
- The working weekly meal rotation was intended as an editable **example**, never proof of vitamin/mineral sufficiency.
- Beware "healthy"/"chemical-free" marketing. A plain frozen product is not inherently less nutritious than fresh. Prioritize regulated sources, handling, label accuracy and credible exposure risks.
- 2026 nutrition/diet research discussed nutrient-optimized menus, DASH, Mediterranean-style patterns, time-restricted eating, and diet replacement. Study populations, follow-up durations and endpoints differ; cross-study comparisons do not prove one menu best.
- Future changes should distinguish product label, database composition, user-confirmed package, and assumption.

## Working estimates, not intake facts
By V4.9, the *sample* weekly menu's label scenarios gave about **1,826–1,840 kcal/day** and **161–162g protein/day**, conditional on mackerel and NutriSoy label versions. An older active overlay quoted approximately 1,836 kcal and 163.4g. These are model scenarios, not daily observations; they must not be silently merged.
No validated 13-vitamin / complete mineral adequacy calculation exists.

## Limitations and blockers
- Exact branded matches incomplete; product variants change nutrition.
- NutriSoy Omega (manufacturer vs retailer) and Ayam mackerel (manufacturer vs retailer) panels differ; verify actual packaging.
- Existing model requires *measured cooked kailan yield* before full week's cost may be reported.
- 26 nutrient fields still had incomplete coverage in V4.9.
- Old QA passed normal inputs while accepting negative calories/protein/price, unsupported units, or implausible maintenance. These were repaired in later versions but not all mutation tests repeated on every overlay.
- Database record IDs and links are identifiers, not proof that data match the consumed food.
- No automatic private-log sync or nutrition-to-app import is authorized.

## Scope
Historical price findings from November 2026 may already be expired. This is a research log, not a live shopping quotation, clinical recommendation, or guarantee of weight loss.
