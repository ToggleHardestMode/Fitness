## Retail-price audit rules
- Store effective date and expiration date; a November 12 promotion is not valid on November 13.
- Use **price / edible gram** or **price / 30g protein**, never conflate pack and edible quantities.
- Separate online prices from in-store offers and delivery.
- Label manufacturer vs retailer price claims and package variants separately.
- Avoid zero filling on unknown cost.
- Keep purchased raw weight, edible weight and cooked yield fields distinct.
